About Travix

Travix is a modern tourism and hospitality startup founded by passionate young entrepreneurs with a vision to transform travel experiences across India. We aim to make travel planning simple, affordable, organized, and memorable for every traveler.

Our platform provides complete travel solutions including hotel bookings, transport arrangements, curated local experiences, guided tours, corporate travel management, and personalized tourism services. We combine smart digital tools with real human support to ensure a smooth and stress-free journey.

Travix strongly believes in community-based and sustainable tourism. We collaborate with local businesses, hosts, and service providers to promote regional culture, generate employment, and contribute to economic growth in different parts of the country.

Our mission is to deliver quality travel experiences while maintaining transparency, safety, affordability, and customer satisfaction. We focus on building trust and long-term relationships with our clients through reliable service and personalized attention.


Our Vision

To become one of India's most trusted tourism platforms by offering innovative, sustainable, and customer-centric travel solutions.
